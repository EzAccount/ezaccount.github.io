PhD candidate at UVA Math, advised by L. Petrov. I study stochastic systems — random permutations, lozenge tilings, stochastic vertex models, polymer models, random walks — whose common feature is integrability, arising from underlying geometric or algebraic structure.

Current focus: applications of vertex models and the Yang–Baxter equation to other areas of mathematics and to other sciences.
